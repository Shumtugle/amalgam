# Changelog

## 1.2.1 — 2026-10-18

The cut, as it was meant to be.

- The cut goes through the crystal. The line on the glass becomes a plane through the metal: everything beyond it is gone, the growing hopper too, and what straddles it keeps a flat face of bare, pale metal that turns with the cluster. New hoppers do not grow past an old cut.
- The piece that falls is the one holding less of the crystal; with no crystal on either side, the smaller one. Cutting beside the crystal still costs nothing.
- The world is a slab now. The piece that goes tips over the cut and drops into the black; the piece that stays turns over on the cut until its edge faces you, showing the strata of the surfaces below; the next one spreads out from the cut, and the crystal rises through it.
- A low grinding as the world turns over.
- The eye cuts beside the crystal and through it, in turn.
- Cuts are saved with the game.

## 1.2 — 2026-10-18

The cut.

- One straight stroke from edge to edge cuts the world in two. The smaller piece darkens and falls away for good, taking the drops and hoppers on it; qubits in lost hoppers are gone too. The edge that stays shows the strata of the surfaces beneath, then the next surface washes across.
- Seven surfaces in turn: clear, lilac, light blue, lime, violet, blue, red. Drops and reflections take the surface's colour. Strings show only on the clear surface and on those that hear them.
- Lilac, violet and red listen to strings, not to looks: a pattern there is an order of plucks on the breath, repeated. The other surfaces listen to looks, as before.
- A cycle ends only after patterns on two different surfaces.
- The eye solves it the same way: it cuts, makes strings with circles where it needs them, plucks, and cuts again.
- The surface, the surfaces heard and the hoppers lost are saved.

## 1.0 — 2026-10-18

The eye.

- Back opens an eye in the top right corner for a few seconds. Back again while it is open leaves the game.
- Touch the eye and the game plays itself while the eye watches. Your own touches are ignored until it is done; back takes the game back at any moment.

## 0.8 — 2026-10-18

The price of order, and the end of a cycle.

- A heard pattern lowers disorder only by what it actually folds into white: 0.1 for each qubit fused. With nothing to fuse it is sung back and changes nothing.
- The world tires of patterns as a whole, not only of each one: every pattern heard soon after another is worth less, and the weariness fades over minutes.
- A cycle ends when the crystal is nearly all white on still water, or when every pit of a full cluster holds a qubit. The cluster spins faster, shrinks into the lens it bent and is gone, leaving a single drop.
- A touch on the drop begins the next cycle, with every colour shifted.
- The cycle count is saved; a collapse interrupted by closing the game counts as finished.

## 0.7 — 2026-10-18

Strings.

- The lines between entangled qubits are strings now: they tremble as a standing wave, still at the qubits and widest in the middle.
- Touch a string to pluck it. It swings wide, settles, and sounds as a plucked string: a long one low, a short one high.
- Every breath of the crystal stirs the strings a little; a new entangled pair sets its string ringing.

## 0.6 — 2026-10-18

The circle.

- A closed circle drawn on the liquid holds its trace for a few seconds.
- A pair born inside the circle is observed by the circle itself: it becomes real with no touch, cannot get out, and rings against the edge, higher for a smaller circle. Each ring brings a little resonance.
- When the circle fades, both drops fall in together and settle as an entangled pair of qubits, joined by a faint line of light that shows through the metal. With no empty pits, they bind two qubits already there.
- Send one entangled hopper under and its partner goes under in the same instant.
- A long stroke has to be straight to open the edge-on galaxy, so a circle does not.

## 0.5 — 2026-10-18

The deep.

- A touch on a hopper sends it down into the black liquid, darkening as it goes, with its qubit. Half a turn of the cluster later it rises again.
- Visions of deep space open through the liquid now and then, bent by its ripples, and fade after a few seconds: a jump to light speed, a half-lit planet before a blue galaxy, a supernova, a lava world, a nebula, a star with crossed rays, a galaxy seen edge-on.
- Each vision belongs to an event and opens only by chance, with a quiet spell after each: a heard pattern, a hopper going under or coming back, disorder at its peak, three looks at nothing, four looks on the breath, a long stroke across the surface, or nothing at all.
- Everything is computed; there are still no image files.

## 0.4 — 2026-10-17

Escape from entropy.

- Disorder creeps up with time and with every qubit and hopper. It brings more pairs, a surface that ripples by itself, a string that drifts out of tune and beats, and a film that shimmers and fades.
- The world listens to the order of observations: the side of the crystal each drop was on, and whether it was looked at on the breath. A run of three or four played twice in a row is a pattern.
- A heard pattern is sung back. The newest qubits fold into one white qubit, the spectrum clock steps back, disorder drops and the surface stills.
- A pattern once heard earns nothing for a while: the world gets used to it. New ones have to be found.
- Disorder and white qubits are saved.

## 0.3 — 2026-10-17

Space and qubits.

- Every finished hopper opens an empty pit. A drop that falls past the horizon settles into the next empty pit as a qubit: a small cube of its own light.
- Qubits take their colour from the spectrum in the order they arrive, red first, violet last: the crystal shows its own history.
- Each new qubit rings the next note of a scale that climbs with the spectrum.
- Drops are bigger and easier to touch.

## 0.2 — 2026-10-17

The observer.

- Pairs of quicksilver drops rise where the waves pile up, and on every breath.
- An unobserved pair sinks back. Touching a drop makes the pair real: one drop spirals into the crystal and feeds it, the other escapes, leaving a wake, and is heard as resonance.
- An observation on the breath counts twice. A touch on empty liquid unsettles nearby pairs and brings nothing.
- The crystal's mass bends the liquid round it like a lens.
- The string sinks with resonance and thickens with mass.
- The game is saved when closed and grows a little while away.
- Touches no longer feed the crystal directly.

## 0.1 — 2026-10-17

First playable slice.

- Black liquid with ripples from every touch and slide.
- The first touch plants a crystal; touches feed it, and it grows hopper by hopper out of its own faces.
- The crystal breathes every 2.6 seconds; a touch within a fifth of a second of the breath resonates.
- Bells struck under water, a low string that deepens with the crystal's mass.
- Shaped vibration: a heavy hit, a gap, a low tail.
